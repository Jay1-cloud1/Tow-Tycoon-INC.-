# Release changes

## v0.2.0

- **Overflow-safe tile math (CodeQL fix)**: tile-grid sizes and indexes are computed in `std::size_t` via `simulation::tileCount` and `simulation::tileIndex` (`src/simulation/TileIndex.h`), so a large map can no longer overflow `int` before it is used as a container size or index.
- **Combined PC and GitHub builds**: brings the v0.1.1 features below together with the v0.1.0 pre-release work: a main menu with Continue, New Game (asks before replacing a save), Settings and Quit, a fullscreen toggle (F11, or F in Settings), the version shown on the menu and HUD, the README, and the Windows build and release workflow.


## v0.1.1

- **Public Share Selling & Buybacks**: Enabled public company share trading. Once a company completes an IPO (`isPublic == true`), players can sell 5% tranches of company shares (`S` key in Stock panel) for liquid cash proceeds based on real-time market share valuation, or execute share buybacks (`B` key) to increase company equity ownership.
- **Order Volume Scaling & Fleet Starvation Fix**: Overhauled order dispatch generation to prevent cars piling up in contract lots and fleet trucks idling. Call order targets dynamically scale up to 48 concurrent jobs based on fleet size, driver headcount, contracted storage lots, and adverse weather. Every tow delivery by player, automated fleet, or rival triggers instant order replenishment. `fleetMaxClass()` allows jobs for all owned truck classes (medium, heavy, roadside) to spawn regardless of the active truck.
- **10x Terrain Expansion (256x192)**: Expanded the simulation map to 256x192 tiles (49,152 tiles total, ~10.2x larger than the legacy 80x60 grid). `SaveGameCodec` and world initialization dynamically support large-world terrain while maintaining full backward-compatibility with legacy save fixtures.
- **Named Cities & Regional Geography**: Added six distinct regional municipalities across the expanded map: `Towburg Central`, `Pinehurst Valley`, `Oakridge Heights`, `Port Towton` (with coastal harbor & waterfront), `Sunnyside Commercial`, and `Ironwood Industrial`. Real-time spatial resolution detects and displays the active city in the HUD, minimap, and dispatch tracker.
- **OpenTTD-Style Landscape & City Growth**: Implemented an OpenTTD-inspired landscape layout with inter-city arterial highways, waterways, coastal harbor, and scenic forested corridors. Municipalities simulate population, prosperity, and growth levels that advance weekly based on tow delivery volume.
- **Dense Urban Blocks & Recreational Parks**: Transformed city layouts into dense wall-to-wall building blocks without dead space, balanced by dedicated recreational parks featuring perimeter sidewalks, central water fountains, and shade trees.
- **Developer Mode Landscape Controls (OpenTTD-Style)**: Added developer mode toggled by `F1` or `` ` `` (tilde/backquote) for AI agents and rapid design. Provides interactive mouse-click painting and a scriptable C++ API: Build Road (`1`), Demolish/Bulldoze (`2`), Place Building & Lot (`3`), Place Recreational Park (`4`), Plant Trees (`5`), Place Water (`6`), Place Sand (`7`), Found City (`8`), and Spawn Call (`9`).
- **Complete UI/UX Makeover**: Redesigned the in-game environment layout with a modern dark-slate aesthetic: top status header with live IPO badge, cash, calendar, weather, and current municipality; floating mission/dispatch card with destination distance; bottom command action dock; regional radar minimap; and developer mode toolbar.
- **Save/Load & Replay Continuity**: Preserves towed cargo, dispatches, AI trips, RNG state, weather, timers, and credit history across save/load. Reads save versions 1–6 and writes version 7. Automatic `.bak` backup retention and atomic save protection.
- **OpenTTD Graphics Download & Engine Asset Pipeline**: Downloaded official OpenTTD / OpenGFX sprite sheets into `Assets/openttd/` (`gui04.png`, `graphs.png`, `help.png`, `vehiclesroad01.png`, `flatbeds.png`, `1456_1457_parks.png`, `base-1421.1425.1424-offices.png`, `base-1426-1429-flats.png`, `ToyRdDep.png`, `infra06.png`). Integrated `openttdGuiTex` in `Engine` for high-fidelity UI icons, town halls, and evaluation trophies with safe destruction in `Engine::shutdown()`.
- **Town Directory & Local Authority Council Relations**: Added OpenTTD-style Local Authority system and interactive Town Directory panel (`P_TOWNS`, key `H` or `Y`). Each regional municipality tracks council standing ratings (-1,000 to +1,000) from *Appalling* to *Outstanding*. Delivering tows boosts local council relations (+20) while bulldozing penalizes (-40). Players can fund new building construction (`B`), sponsor municipal road resurfacing (`R`), bid on Exclusive Service Contracts (`X`) granting +25% payout bonuses, or attempt council bribery (`C`) with risk of whistleblower scandal and fines.
- **1,000-Point Detailed Company Performance Rating Matrix**: Added OpenTTD-inspired performance evaluation window (`P_PERF`, key `P`) benchmarking company operations across 7 categories (Fleet Vehicles, Contract Lots, Total Tows, Weekly Net Profit, Valuation, Debt Clearance, and Council Favor) awarding company ranks up to *Tycoon*.
- **OpenTTD Breaking News Wire Ticker**: Implemented live newspaper banner wire broadcasting municipal expansions, civic infrastructure projects, exclusive contracts, and municipal ethics probes.
- **Gameplay Regressions & Test Suite**: Added dedicated regression coverage in `tests/gameplay_regression_test.cpp` verifying 10x terrain, regional cities, share selling/buyback mechanics, developer mode tools, Town Directory navigation and council actions, Performance Rating matrix, and rendering across all viewports alongside full replay determinism.
